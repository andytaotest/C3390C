Workshop booking change request

Successful bookings reduce availability. Requests exceeding capacity are rejected. Cancelling an active booking restores its seats.

R-CANCEL: Introduce a 24-hour cancellation cutoff. Clearly late cancellations must be rejected: keep the booking active, leave availability unchanged and display Cancellation closes 24 hours before the workshop. Clearly early cancellations still work as before.

Confirm the rule at exactly 24 hours and the authoritative clock and time zone. Until then, use clearly early or late cases. The project has 30 minutes of shared staging access.
