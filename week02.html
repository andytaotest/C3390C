L04 Activity 1

What question is the check answering?

Core guided discussion: Q1/Q2/Q3/Q6. Identify their objectives and static/dynamic or manual/automated dimensions. Q4/Q5 will be explored through the lab after the break. Completing a full six-card written classification is optional.

  1. Q1. A person books two places through the full product and checks that the no-code total is S$40.
  2. Q2. An observer watches a first-time organiser complete a booking task in the running product, noting confusion and completion.
  3. Q3. A team reads the requirement "The booking page must be fast and easy" and identifies missing criteria before running any software.
  4. Q4. After a claimed capacity repair, a person repeats the reported four-place failure to check that it is corrected.
  5. Q5. After a capacity change, a script reruns a previously passing STUDENT discount assertion to look for unwanted effects.
  6. Q6. A unit-test runner executes both outcomes of the capacity guard and collects branch coverage.

Extension: Propose a measurable replacement for "fast and easy". State the task, target, and environment or intended users. The stakeholder must agree any proposed targets.
