L04 Activity 1

What question is the check answering?

After the break, begin L04 with Q1/Q2/Q3/Q6. Identify their objectives and static/dynamic or manual/automated dimensions. Q4/Q5 will be explored through A5. Completing a full six-card written classification is optional.

  1. Q1. A person books two places through the full product and checks that the no-code total is S$40.
  2. Q2. An observer watches a first-time organiser complete a booking task in the running product, noting confusion and completion.
  3. Q3. A team reads the requirement "The booking page must be fast and easy" and identifies missing criteria before running any software.
  4. Q4. After a claimed capacity repair, a person repeats the reported four-place failure to check that it is corrected.
  5. Q5. After a capacity change, a script reruns a previously passing STUDENT discount assertion to look for unwanted effects.
  6. Q6. A unit-test runner executes both outcomes of the capacity guard and collects branch coverage.

Extension: Propose a measurable replacement for "fast and easy". State the task, target, and environment or intended users. The stakeholder must agree any proposed targets.

L04 Activity 3

Where would automation help?

Individually consider all four scenarios M1-M4. For each, choose automate, human-led, or a combination, and give a reason about repetition, stable expected results, human judgment, or maintenance. These are four task scenarios to evaluate, not four separate runnable tools.

  • M1. Repeat stable price and capacity checks after every change.
  • M2. Observe a new organiser finding confusing wording.
  • M3. Check hundreds of stable invalid-input combinations.
  • M4. Evaluate a changing prototype with no settled workflow.

After recording your A5 evidence and decisions for M1-M4, select B and run repeated checks. Compare the detailed results with your manual observations.
